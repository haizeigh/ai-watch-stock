Quick Start Guide

1. Add your stocks
Click ⚙️ Settings → In the "Stocks" section, type a stock symbol (e.g. AAPL) and click "+ Add". Repeat for each stock you want to track. You can also set your Finnhub API key in Settings.

2. Find your stocks in the document
Each stock appears with its company symbol in the document. Next to it you'll see:
Price — the current stock price (e.g. 185.23)
Change — how much it changed (e.g. -0.77%)

3. Switch disguise styles
Click ⚙️ Settings → "Disguise Style" to change how the document looks. Choose from: Competitive Analysis Report, Release Notes, Project Report, Terminal Log, or JSON API Response.

4. Change language
In Settings, pick English, 한국어, or 简体中文. The document text updates to match.

5. Everything auto-saves
All your settings, stocks, and preferences are saved in your browser. Next time you open this page, everything is exactly as you left it.

💡 Tip: The page title and favicon match the disguise style. No one will know you're checking stock prices!
